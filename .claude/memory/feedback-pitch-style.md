Live pitches open with a hook. The lead story is that people now ask Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini where to stay, and Stumari makes independent hotels findable and bookable by AI, paid in stablecoins. Keep tech detail (Anchor, AUC, E2E counts, MCP tool lists) out of the talk and save it for Q&A. Don't use "Stripe has never supported Georgia". The founders say Georgia has plenty of other payment options, so it's a weak argument. Card-blocked visitors can still get one line.

**Why:** On 2026-10-14 Giorgi rejected the first 3-minute deck as too technical, not catchy enough, and wrong on Stripe. He sees AI search plus AI booking as the biggest opening for hotels and for crypto.

**How to apply:** For decks, pitches and one-pagers, lead with the AI-booking shift, then the hotel's pain (15–25% commission, 27% cancellations), then Stumari, then why stablecoins ("money a machine can send, terms a machine can trust"). The honesty rules in [[project-stumari]] still apply: don't claim to be first at AI booking (Travala, Google AI Mode exist), no invented AI-usage stats, and never say Georgian hotels can legally take USDC.
