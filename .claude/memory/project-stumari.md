**Legal blocker (2026-10-01):** docs/research/crypto-legality-georgia.md. Organic Law on the NBG Art. 39¹ (verified on matsne) prohibits paying with virtual assets, stablecoins included, outside NBG-defined VASP cases. Direct USDC into a hotel's wallet conflicts with a plain reading; the lawful pattern is a registered VASP settling the hotel in GEL (CityPay, Keepz/AEON Pay). Stumari itself may be a VASP. Decision on the settlement model is pending with the user; recommend no real-money Georgian pilot until a lawyer confirms.
